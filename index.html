ACM Uploader

ACM Uploader is a personal desktop tool made and used by HaydenB. It runs only on its owner's own Windows PC. It edits the owner's own gameplay recordings into short vertical videos and posts them to the owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in to TikTok with TikTok Login Kit and gives the app permission to post.
  2. The app edits a gameplay recording into a short video on the owner's PC.
  3. The owner reviews and approves the video and its caption.
  4. The app uploads the video to the owner's TikTok account with the Content Posting API. Posts are private by default.

The app is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.
