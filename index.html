ACM Uploader

ACM Uploader is a personal desktop tool made and used by HaydenB. It runs only on its owner's own Windows PC. It edits the owner's own gameplay recordings into videos and Shorts and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner records gameplay on their PC.
  2. The app edits each recording into short vertical videos (and longer videos for long recordings) and writes a title, description and hashtags.
  3. The owner signs in once with Google (YouTube Data API) and with TikTok (Login Kit) and gives the app permission to upload.
  4. The app uploads the videos to the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API v3) and TikTok account (Content Posting API), spread out across the day on a schedule the owner controls. The owner can pause, reorder or remove any video.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner's own accounts are connected, and no one else can sign in to it.
