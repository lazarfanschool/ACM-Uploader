ACM Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

ACM Uploader is a personal desktop tool made and used only by HaydenB ("the owner"). Everything it stores stays on the owner's own computer. It has no servers and no analytics, and it never sells or shares data.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, username) so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Sign-in tokens that let the app post on the owner's behalf.
  • Post status (for example, whether an upload finished or failed).

How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI) on the owner's computer, and only that Windows user account can decrypt them. They are never written to logs or sent anywhere except to TikTok.

What is sent to TikTok

Only the videos and captions the owner chooses to post, and the requests needed to sign in and check post status.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Clicking Disconnect in the app deletes the stored tokens and asks TikTok to revoke the app's access. Access can also be removed in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
