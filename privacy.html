ACM Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

ACM Uploader is a personal desktop tool made and used only by HaydenB ("the owner"). Everything it stores stays on the owner's own computer. It has no servers and no analytics, and it never sells or shares data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy, and use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service.

  • What the app accesses: the connected channel's ID and title (to show which channel is connected), and the ability to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • What the app stores: a Google refresh token, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI) on the owner's computer, plus the IDs and links of videos it uploaded. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google.
  • How to revoke access: click Disconnect in the app (it asks Google to revoke the token and deletes it), or remove the app at Google security settings.

TikTok

  • What the app accesses: basic profile information (open ID, display name, username) to show which account is connected; the ability to upload videos to the owner's TikTok account or drafts; and the status of those uploads.
  • What the app stores: TikTok sign-in tokens, encrypted with DPAPI on the owner's computer, and the IDs of uploads it made.
  • How to revoke access: click Disconnect in the app, or in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

What is sent to the platforms

Only the videos, titles, descriptions and captions the owner's app uploads, and the requests needed to sign in and check upload status.

Sharing and retention

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Tokens are kept only while an account is connected and are deleted on Disconnect. Upload records stay in the local database on the owner's computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
